# Tonone

> Research status: **Source-level** · Lifecycle: **active** · Last reviewed: **2026-08-12**

Tonone is a general multi-team agent plugin whose Design surface is distributed across a dedicated design bundle and product/full-team workflows. Color, layout, typography, motion, prototyping, brand, illustration, accessibility, tokens and content specialists can audit source or produce a bounded specification for another team.

## Reconnaissance and handoff prevent context-free design

Design skills generally separate inspection (`recon`) from a proposed palette, layout, motion system, prototype or token architecture. Product bundles add wireframe and implementation-handoff skills; the full team preserves those records while engineering and delivery agents act on the repository. The codebase and accepted specs remain authority rather than a hidden Tonone canvas.

Team bundles are distribution slices of one maintained system, not independent product lineages. The repository also credits Open Design and several known upstream skill libraries; those dependencies remain their own identities and are not silently reattributed to Tonone.
