# 3D Modeling Skill

> Research status: **Source-level** · Lifecycle: **active** · Last reviewed: **2026-08-12**

3D Modeling Skill defines two auditable routes from language, measurements and photos to a print-ready model: a certified-template path for bounded jobs and a five-role file-contract path for off-template reconstruction and assemblies.

## Consequence changes review, not route eligibility

The deterministic route selects one of a small certified template domains, freezes and hashes a job contract, builds geometry and writes a receipt. An inconsequential in-domain job can complete without model dispatch; a consequential one requires a bounded safety response. Work outside the certified domain is routed to fitted or full review rather than quietly downgraded.

The full route separates metrology, design, verification, print engineering and orchestration through files. Review packets are written before the process stops, and a response is supplied by rerunning the same command. Independent reviewers do not receive one another's conclusions, reducing anchoring.

Contracts and Python/model source carry intent; the kernel result and measurement reports carry geometric evidence; export files are deliveries. The receipt states what was not checked, making absence of proof part of the artifact rather than hidden operational knowledge.
