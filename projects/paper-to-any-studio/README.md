# Paper to Any Studio / Nexus

> Research status: **Architecture-level** · Lifecycle: **active** · Last reviewed: **2026-08-12**

Paper to Any Studio (also presented through the Nexus desktop workspace) is the hosted commercial product connected to—but explicitly not identical with—the open-source Paper2Any repository. It provides a unified Studio, persistent Spaces, Labs applications, online Office previews, credits and long-running workflows.

## Why this is a separate product record

The [Paper2Any first-party README](https://github.com/OpenDCAI/Paper2Any/blob/1ca1f658e811dcec4f9e3d951ce8dacd344d050c/README.md) states that the online product is not the same implementation and that the complete commercial source is private. Its ordinary-user loop is broader than the open repository: organize work in Spaces, launch specialist Labs, inspect generated Office artifacts in the workspace, and return to asynchronous jobs.

The public [desktop releases repository](https://github.com/DeepThinkingZhouLiu/Paper2Any-Desktop-Releases) distributes compiled Nexus installers but does not reveal the internal artifact model or runtime. The hosted and Feishu product surfaces were access-blocked during review. Consequently this dossier records only first-party architecture and workflow claims; it does not infer source-level mechanisms.
