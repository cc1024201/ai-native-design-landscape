# MindGraph

> Research status: **Source-level** · Lifecycle: **active** · Last reviewed: **2026-08-12**

MindGraph defines AI-assisted design as learning through structured maps. Circle, Bubble, Tree, Concept, and mind-map forms encode different reasoning activities rather than interchangeable visual themes.

## Pedagogy shapes the graph vocabulary

A prompt is converted into map-specific nodes and relationships, then materialized on an interactive Vue canvas. Teachers and learners can rearrange, expand, and refine the result. The structured graph is therefore the work object; the black-cat visual language is a product experience around it.

## Human play follows generation

The ordinary loop is describe a topic, receive a structured map, manipulate it alone or with classmates, and share the result. That makes AI a starting-point generator rather than the sole author. FastAPI services provide the generation and project boundary behind the client.

## What is distinctive—and what is not proven

MindGraph is included because its map semantics and classroom workflow represent visual coordination, not because it has a themed UI. Source proves the editable learning surface; educational effectiveness and real classroom collaboration quality still require user evidence.
