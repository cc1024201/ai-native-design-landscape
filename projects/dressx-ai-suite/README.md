## Studio production is narrower than fashion product design

DRESSX AI Studio is a self-serve surface where a brand uploads tops bottoms full looks and shoes defines creative direction and runs bulk e-commerce image or video production. It can create a seasonal collection's campaign media without a conventional photo shoot.

The broader AI Suite also exposes virtual try-on avatars customer and stylist assistants retail mirrors drop-in widgets and REST APIs. Those surfaces use visual results in shopping journeys but they are not counted as separate Design-authoring teams. Their authority is a customer's commerce session or brand integration not a second editable garment model.

The evidence supports generated media and batch-job continuity but not a structured pattern 3D garment or technical pack. “Digital fashion” should therefore not be read as proof that AI Studio authors the product's manufacturing definition. Public material also does not disclose edit history generation parameters asset schemas or campaign versioning.
