# Atlas 3D Design Team

> Research status: **Architecture-level** · Lifecycle: **active** · Last reviewed: **2026-08-12**

Atlas 3D Design is a second independently installable Atlas team for modeling, rigging, animation, texturing, VTuber work and real-time asset optimization. Seven agents and domain skills route work across Blender, Unity, Omniverse and VRoid rather than claiming one universal 3D source format.

## Host-native files retain authority

The orchestrator and team manifest assign analysis, construction and optimization stages; a logger records tool use and handoffs. Blender scenes, Unity assets or VRoid projects remain authoritative in their respective hosts, and exported meshes or animations are downstream delivery objects.

Public source exposes detailed team and skill contracts but not an independent scene runtime or cross-host persistence model. The dossier therefore stops at architecture level and counts the component separately from the unrelated teams in the top-level marketplace.
