# OWASP ThreatAtlas

> Research status: **Source-level** · Lifecycle: **active** · Last reviewed: **2026-08-12**

ThreatAtlas defines architecture design as collaborative threat modeling: a data-flow diagram is inseparable from threats, mitigations, ownership, risk, approval, and changing history.

## Security semantics live on the graph

Processes, stores, external entities, flows, and trust boundaries form native DFD state. Threat and mitigation records attach to those objects and can draw from structured knowledge bases. Heatmaps and analytics are views over the resulting risk model, not independent design artifacts.

## AI assists inside a governed workflow

An optional administrator-configured assistant helps explore threats and proposals. It does not bypass role-based access, acceptance justification, review dashboards, or ownership. Formal approval and risk acceptance distinguish a suggestion from an organizational decision.

## Versioning includes meaning

The product records diagram history and threat/mitigation changes, supports comments and notifications, and can expose a CI/CD security gate. Real-time diagram sync and live cursors serve collaboration; they do not make every AI suggestion automatically safe or complete.
