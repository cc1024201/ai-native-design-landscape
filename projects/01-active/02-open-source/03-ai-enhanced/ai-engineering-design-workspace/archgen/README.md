## Generation and physics are presented as one loop

ArchGen's preview describes natural-language requirements becoming a feature- and constraint-bearing parametric model. Changes to the prompt iterate dimensions fillets and constraints. A Physora layer is presented as running structural stress fluid and thermal inference during generation while DFM tolerance and standards checks constrain the output.

The final model can be exported to STEP IGES STL or named native CAD formats. STEP and native representations may continue as editable engineering geometry; STL is a mesh boundary.

## Evidence caution

This is an early-access product claim rather than a source-inspected solver. The first-party page establishes a team product boundary and intended ordinary workflow but not benchmark artifacts equations error bounds geometry kernel or an accessible editor. Claims such as exact performance multipliers standards compliance and production readiness remain unverified positioning.
