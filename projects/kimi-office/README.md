# Kimi Docs, Sheets and Slides

> Research status: **Architecture-level / closed-source boundary reached** · Lifecycle: **active** · Last reviewed: **2026-08-12**

Moonshot AI's Kimi office surfaces turn natural-language requests and uploaded files into Word, PDF, spreadsheet and presentation artifacts. Users can preview in the browser, refine generated work and download native `.docx`, `.xlsx` or editable `.pptx` files.

## Three editors share one Kimi Agent boundary

Docs supports generation, reformatting, comments and revision suggestions; Sheets constructs formulas, tables, charts and multi-sheet workbooks; Slides accepts documents, images and templates, creates native charts and diagrams and permits element-level online editing before export. These are modes of the Kimi product suite, not three independently attributed teams.

Public first-party evidence establishes the user loop and native delivery formats, but not the internal graph, model-to-editor protocol, version history or persistence implementation. A community repository that says it extracted Kimi document skills is therefore treated as an unofficial distribution of this product lineage rather than a second invention.
